About

I’m Mustafa Arslan, a software architect and applied AI researcher based in Istanbul. I design high-performance, reliable software systems and explore how agentic AI, graph intelligence, and neuro-symbolic architectures can solve complex real-world problems.

My career spans enterprise software, cloud-native platforms, payments, aviation, telecommunications, and fraud detection. I enjoy working at the boundary between deep technical problem-solving and practical product impact: turning ambiguous problems into clear architectures, resilient systems, and tools that make complex decisions easier.

Outside of my professional work, I build and publish independent research projects on long-horizon AI agents, AI memory systems, retrieval architectures, and intelligent financial systems. This site is where I share my writing, research, conference notes, and the ideas I am still working through.
